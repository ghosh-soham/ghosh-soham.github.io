About

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at the University of Washington, Seattle, advised by Max Lieblich and Farbod Shokrieh. My research is supported by a Simons Dissertation Fellowship in Mathematics.

I am interested in algebraic geometry with an arithmetic flavour and in tropical geometry. Before coming to Seattle, I completed my Bachelor of Mathematics (Honors) at the Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore.

Here is my CV (last updated October 2026). My talks and conference travel and my teaching and mentoring are listed on separate pages.

Research interests

  • Algebraic geometry with an arithmetic flavour: Brauer groups, Azumaya algebras, twisted sheaves, moduli and stacks, K-theory.
  • Tropical and non-archimedean geometry: intersection theory of non-archimedean cycles and currents, tropical cohomology, tropical abelian varieties, and Fourier–Mukai transforms.
  • Birational geometry: positivity conditions on bundles and foliations, and the resulting characterizations of special Fano varieties.
  • Commutative algebra arising from algebraic geometry.
