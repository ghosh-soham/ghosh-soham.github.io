Mentoring and outreach

Mentor, Washington Directed Reading Program · University of Washington, 2026–present

The Washington Directed Reading Program pairs undergraduates with graduate student mentors for a quarter of guided independent reading. In Spring 2026 I mentored Larry Wen on commutative algebra, following Atiyah and Macdonald’s Introduction to Commutative Algebra, and Max Han on representation theory, following Serre’s Linear Representations of Finite Groups.

Instructor, Husky Math Academy · University of Washington, 2024–2025

I taught weekly problem-solving and discussion sessions for students in grades 7–9, covering a range of advanced topics beyond the school curriculum and designing the classroom activities for each session.

Service

I have refereed for Communications in Algebra, the Journal of Commutative Algebra, the Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics, the American Mathematical Monthly, and the Indian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics.
